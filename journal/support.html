Eltheia Journal Support

Email support@eltheia.com and a person will reply, usually within two working days. Telling us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong helps us fix it faster. We cannot see your entries, so please describe the problem in your own words.

Common questions

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

On the old phone, open Settings > Backup and export a backup file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, open Settings > Backup and restore from that file. A full iPhone transfer or iCloud Backup also carries the app's data across.

Can I bring my history from another mood tracker?

Yes. Export your entries from the other app as a CSV or backup file, save it to Files, then open Settings > Import history and pick the file. You see a preview before anything is added, and days you have already logged are never overwritten.

How do I add my own activities, groups or sections?

Settings > Activities adds, renames, reorders and regroups activities. Settings > Sections adds lists such as To do, Gratitude or your own. Removing an activity keeps it on the past days where you logged it.

My reminder does not show up.

Open the iPhone Settings app, find Eltheia Journal under Notifications and allow notifications, then set the time again in the app under Settings > Reminder. A Focus mode can also silence reminders.

How do I delete everything?

Settings > Erase all data, or delete the app. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Is this a medical app?

No. Eltheia Journal is a personal diary. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. If you are struggling, please talk to a doctor or someone you trust.
