Eltheia Journal Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Eltheia Journal is made by Eltheia Inc. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app. The short version: it stays on your iPhone, and we never see it.

What the app stores

Your moods, the activities you tap, any times, lengths and notes you add, your daily lists (such as To do and Gratitude), your activity and mood settings, and your reminder time. All of it is stored in a database inside the app on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it does not send your entries, your settings or anything about how you use it to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services that receive your data.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules the reminder on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server to make this work.

Backups and imports

When you export a backup, the app creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet. You decide where the file goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive or another app. Once it is there, that place's own privacy terms apply. When you restore a backup or import your history from a file you exported from another mood app, the app reads the file you pick and processes it on your phone. Nothing from the file is sent anywhere.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. That is controlled by your iPhone settings and Apple's terms, not by us.

Links to our website

Settings has two links, Privacy Policy and Support, which open pages on eltheia.com in an in-app browser. The app sends none of your entries when you open them. As with any website, your phone's browser connects to the site to load the page. eltheia.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising; our host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs for a short time to run and secure the site.

Deleting your data

Settings > Erase all data deletes everything the app stored. Deleting the app does the same. Backup files you exported are not touched; delete them wherever you saved them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever needs to send data off your phone, this policy will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@eltheia.com. Eltheia Inc. is responsible for personal information under Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA).
