Apps

Two apps, both quiet.

Eltheon

An e-reader for the EPUB, PDF, Markdown and text files you already own, on your phone, tablet and laptop. It works offline with no account. An optional plan keeps your library and your place in every book in step across devices. Coming soon.

Braid

Short, playful practice for young children learning letters, sounds and their first numbers, built on how early reading and maths are actually learned. In development.
