Calm software for reading and numbers.

Eltheia Inc. is a small studio in Toronto. We make tools for literacy and numeracy, from a child's first letters and numbers with Braid to a lifetime of books with Eltheon.

Made for every kind of mind

We design with neurodivergent readers and learners in mind: Lexend for easier reading, soft colour, steady layouts, and nothing that flashes, nags or competes for attention.

Does what it needs to

No ads, no feeds, no streaks. Each screen has one job, does it well, and gets out of the way.

Grounded in research

Built on the science of reading and on how children come to understand number, one small step at a time.
